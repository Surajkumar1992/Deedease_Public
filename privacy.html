Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: we only collect what you type into our waitlist or early-access form, we use it only to contact you about Deedease's launch and pilot program, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it anytime by emailing info@deedease.com.

1. Who we are

Deedease ("we", "us") operates deedease.com. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), Deedease is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data you submit on this website. You can reach us at info@deedease.com.

2. What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address only.
  • Early-access / pilot form: your name, email and role (required), and optionally your phone number, city, company and a short note on what you want to verify.
  • Technical data: when you visit, our hosting and font providers automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type (see section 5). We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of our own.

We do not ask for, and you should not submit, sensitive information such as ID numbers, financial details or property documents through these forms.

3. Why we use it

We use your data only, and only with your consent, to:

  • notify you when Deedease launches and send occasional early-access updates;
  • contact you about joining our early-access or pilot program, if you used that form;
  • understand which kinds of users (buyers, brokers, lenders, etc.) are interested, so we can plan the pilot.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data for anyone else's marketing.

4. Consent

By clicking "Join the early-access waitlist", or by ticking the consent box and submitting the early-access form, you give us your consent to process your data for the purposes above. Consent is voluntary, and you can withdraw it at any time as easily as you gave it (see section 7). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run this website. They process data on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms: receives form submissions and delivers them to our inbox.
  • Our email provider: stores the resulting emails in the info@deedease.com mailbox.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website and may keep standard server logs (e.g. IP address).
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash: serve the font and background image, and receive your IP address when your browser loads them.

Some of these providers may store or process data outside India. Where this happens, we rely on transfers permitted under the DPDP Act and choose providers with appropriate security safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist and early-access data until Deedease launches and for up to 12 months after launch, or until you withdraw consent, whichever is earlier. After that, we delete it unless you sign up for the product or the law requires us to keep it longer.

7. Your rights and how to opt out

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erase your data and withdraw consent at any time;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity;
  • grievance redressal: raise a complaint with us about how we handle your data.

To opt out or make any request, email info@deedease.com with the subject "Privacy request" from the email address you signed up with. You can also reply "unsubscribe" to any email we send you. We will act on your request promptly and in any case within the time required by law.

8. Grievances

If you have a concern about how we handle your personal data, contact our grievance contact at info@deedease.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security

This website is served over HTTPS, and access to collected data is restricted to the Deedease team. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable safeguards to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse, and will notify affected users and authorities of a personal data breach as required by law.

10. Children

This website is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Deedease grows, for example when the full product launches. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify people on the waitlist by email.
